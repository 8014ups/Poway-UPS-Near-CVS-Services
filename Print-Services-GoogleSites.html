USPS Requirements for EDDM & Direct Mail

We prepare your prints to meet all USPS specifications:

  • Size: Minimum 6.125" x 11.5" | Maximum 12" x 15"
  • Thickness: 0.007" to 0.25" (we recommend 100# gloss cover)
  • Addressing: "Postal Customer" required for EDDM
  • Indicia: Upper right corner for bulk mail permit
  • Bundling: Sorted by carrier route, bundled in groups of 50-100
